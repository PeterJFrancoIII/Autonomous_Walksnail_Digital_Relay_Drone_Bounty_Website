## 📖 The Story

### The Problem: The Line-of-Sight Barrier
Imagine flying your FPV drone without ever worrying about losing signal behind an obstacle. Currently, the moment an FPV pilot flies behind a building, a thick tree line, or a mountain, the RF signal degrades or drops entirely. The density of objects on the ground makes true exploration impossible without losing your multi-thousand-dollar rig.

### The Vision
What if we change the angle? If we position an autonomous **Relay Drone** at 400 feet AGL (the maximum FAA limit), the signal density issue vanishes. There is significantly less interference between the pilot and the open airspace above the obstacle than there is trying to blast a signal *through* the obstacle on the ground level.

This project will fund the ultimate open-source bounty to crack the **"Holy Grail" of civilian RF networks**: An autonomous, cross-band, 1-hour hovering relay drone that provides 2-square-miles of unbroken Walksnail/ELRS coverage.

### Why Crowdfund a Bounty?
We want FPV pilots who desperately *need* this capability to fund the brilliant engineers and builders who can *create* it. By pooling our resources, we incentivize the world's best hardware hackers to solve this problem for us. 

And the best part? **Every submission, every line of code, and every 3D printed schematic generated by this contest must be 100% open-source.** When the bounty is claimed, the entire community wins the technology forever.

## 🛠️ The Challenge Specs

To claim the prize, competing builders must hit these extreme constraints:
1. **$2,000 Price Cap:** The final BOM cannot exceed $2,000. It must be attainable.
2. **1-Hour Hover:** The drone must hover for a minimum of 60 continuous minutes.
3. **Cross-Band Payload:** It must bridge Walksnail HD Video (5.8/5.2GHz) and ExpressLRS Control (900MHz/2.4GHz) without self-interference.
4. **Autonomous Topographical Brain:** It must automatically take off, land, monitor its own battery, and dynamically reposition itself in 3D space using digital elevation maps to avoid terrain while keeping both the Ground Pilot and the Worker Drone in optimal Line-of-Sight.

## ⚠️ Risks and Challenges

* **Hardware Limitations:** Achieving a 60-minute hover under $2,000 while carrying four active RF modules is extremely difficult. If no single team achieves all requirements perfectly, the judging panel will award the pool to the team that gets the closest, ensuring the knowledge is still open-sourced.
* **Timeline Delays:** Hardware development is messy. Builders may face blown ESCs, fried flight controllers, or supply chain issues. The contest will run for 6 months to allow ample time for prototyping, resetting, and field testing.
* **Regulatory Compliance:** Competitors are strictly instructed to obey all FAA/local regulations during testing (including the 400ft AGL limit and Visual Line of Sight requirements for the relay itself, utilizing spotters if necessary).
